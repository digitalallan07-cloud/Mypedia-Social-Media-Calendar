What's Wrong With Your Child's Legs?

Pediatric

Everyone has an opinion the moment your baby starts standing 👀

Here's what actually matters — and what doesn't.

Bowed legs in babies are completely normal between ages 1 and 2. As long as both legs look the same and your child is growing well, it's part of the process.

But call your pediatrician if you notice:
Bowing that's getting worse after age 2
One leg more bowed than the other
Wider-looking wrists or knees
Late walking or delayed growth

And one more thing — Vitamin D is essential for strong bones. Don't guess the dose. Ask your pediatrician what's right for your child's age.

Does Your Child Walk With Toes Turned Inward?

Pediatric

Your child walks with their toes pointing in — and suddenly everyone's recommending special shoes 🙄

Here's what your pediatrician actually wants you to know.

In-toeing is very common and in most cases causes no pain at all. The cause usually depends on age:
👶 Babies — the foot itself may be slightly curved
🧒 Toddlers — slight rotation in the shin bone
👦 Older children — a natural twist in the thigh bone

The good news? Most children correct naturally as they grow. Special shoes, braces, and exercises are usually not needed.

Watch out if your child:
Is in pain or limping
Has one side noticeably worse than the other
Is not improving with age
