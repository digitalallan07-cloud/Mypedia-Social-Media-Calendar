Why Baby Teeth Matter

Dental

Swipe through why baby teeth matter and how care is decided.

“They fall out anyway. Why treat them?”

Baby teeth help children chew and speak, and help maintain space for permanent teeth. Some baby molars remain until around age 10–12, although timing varies.

Decay can cause pain or infection. Losing a baby tooth early may allow neighbouring teeth to move and affect the space for adult teeth; this does not happen in every case.

The right care depends on the tooth, the extent of decay and your child's needs. Dr. Hasab can explain the options, their benefits and risks, and how to prevent further decay.
